# Privacy Policy for Nomad Rivals

**Effective date:** August 2, 2026  
**Developer:** KajaGames

KajaGames ("KajaGames," "we," "us," or "our") provides the mobile game **Nomad Rivals** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, why it is used, when it may be shared, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Player profile information:** the player name and country/region selection you enter. Your player name and country/region may appear to other players on leaderboards. Do not use your real name or include personal information in your player name.
- **Game choices:** information such as a horse name, selected language, settings, and customization choices. Some of this information is stored only on your device, while game progress and customization information may be synchronized to our cloud service.
- **Support communications:** if you contact us, we receive the information in your message and your contact details.

### Account and gameplay information

- **Anonymous account identifier:** the App creates a unique anonymous Firebase account identifier. The App does not require an email address, password, phone number, or social-media login.
- **Game progress and activity:** levels, experience points, virtual currency, inventory, unlocked maps, tutorial progress, customization state, race counts, wins, race results, best times, leaderboard league/division/rank, rewards, and related timestamps.
- **Purchase information:** product identifiers, localized prices, and purchase status for in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment-card information.

### Information collected automatically

The App and its service providers may automatically collect:

- app interactions and gameplay events, including app launches, races, reward claims, and ad interactions;
- device and account identifiers, including the Firebase installation/user identifier, Android advertising ID (when available), and app set ID;
- device, operating-system, app-version, language, and general technical information;
- IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location such as country or city; and
- diagnostics and performance information, including loading time, errors, crashes or hangs, and energy usage.

### Local storage

The App stores game progress, profile choices, settings, and preferences on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs or similar local storage. Removing the App or clearing its data may delete locally stored information, but it may not delete information already synchronized to our cloud service.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- create and maintain your anonymous game account;
- save and synchronize progress across game sessions;
- operate leaderboards and display player names, country/region selections, and scores;
- provide gameplay, rewards, customization, and in-app purchases;
- serve, measure, personalize where permitted, and protect advertising;
- understand how the App is used and improve gameplay, performance, and reliability;
- detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and invalid advertising activity;
- respond to support requests; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

Where applicable law requires consent, we rely on your consent for personalized advertising or similar processing. In other cases, we process information as needed to provide the App, based on our legitimate interests in operating and securing it, or to comply with law.

## 3. When information is shared

We may share information in the following circumstances:

- **Google Firebase:** Firebase Authentication creates the anonymous account; Cloud Firestore stores cloud saves and leaderboard data; and Firebase Analytics measures App usage. Google may process identifiers, IP addresses, device/app information, interactions, and developer-defined gameplay data.
- **Google AdMob and advertising partners:** the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, Android advertising ID, app set ID, and related identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your region, settings, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.
- **Google Play:** Google Play processes in-app purchases and related transaction information under Google's own privacy policy.
- **Other players:** your chosen player name, country/region selection, leaderboard score, race statistics, rank, league, and division may be visible to other players. Your Firebase account identifier is not intended to be displayed publicly.
- **Legal and safety reasons:** we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or enforce our agreements.
- **Business transfers:** information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate notice and safeguards required by law.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising providers may process information for interest-based advertising where permitted; some privacy laws may define this activity as "sharing" or "targeted advertising."

For more information about Google's practices, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. Data retention

We retain account and cloud-save information while your anonymous account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App. Analytics, advertising, transaction, security, and support records may be retained for the periods configured by the relevant service provider or as needed for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and enforcement. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being overwritten.

We may retain information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it no longer reasonably identifies you. Public leaderboard records may need to be deleted or anonymized separately from the authentication account.

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **Advertising choices:** you can reset or delete your Android advertising ID and manage ad privacy settings in your device settings. Where required, the App or Google's consent interface will provide choices concerning personalized advertising.
- **Device data:** you can clear the App's local storage or uninstall the App. This does not automatically delete cloud data.
- **Profile information:** use a non-identifying player name. Profile information may be changed through available in-game settings.
- **Account and data deletion:** use **Settings > Delete Account** in the App and follow the confirmation steps. You may also request deletion using the contact information below. After verifying and processing a valid request, we will delete or anonymize the cloud profile, game progress, and leaderboard records associated with the anonymous account unless retention is required by law, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record obligations.
- **Privacy rights:** depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent or appeal our response. Contact us to exercise an applicable right.

To help us locate an anonymous account, we may ask for the player name and the in-game account identifier shown in the App. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. Our Google service providers encrypt covered data in transit. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's privacy

Nomad Rivals is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required by local law for independent consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child below that age without legally valid parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to this section, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

If KajaGames later makes the App available to children as a target audience, we will update our practices, advertising configuration, disclosures, and this Privacy Policy as required by the Google Play Families Policy and applicable children's privacy laws.

## 8. International data transfers

KajaGames and its service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we use legally recognized safeguards for international transfers.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our service providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at the same public URL, update the effective date, and provide additional notice in the App when required.
